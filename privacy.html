Privacy Policy

Private by default.

Effective September 11, 2026 · Applies to PerPlan on iPhone, iPad, Mac, Windows, and Android

The short version

PerPlan does not require an account. The app has no advertising, no tracking, no cloud sync, no analytics SDK, and no server of its own. Your plan PDFs, projects, measurements, markups, questions, RFIs, and search indexes are stored only on your device. Nothing you open in PerPlan is sent to us.

Information the app processes

To provide its features, PerPlan processes the PDF files you choose to open, the project names you type, page text extracted from those PDFs (for search, sheet identification, and reference checking), calibration and measurement points, markups, notes, question answers, and RFI drafts. All of this is stored locally in the app's own storage area on your device and never leaves it unless you export it yourself (for example, by sharing an RFI PDF).

What we collect

Nothing. PerPlan does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information, usage data, device identifiers, or document contents. The app makes no network requests during normal use.

Permissions

Files. PerPlan uses the system file picker to open a PDF you choose and to save files you export. It does not scan your storage. Android: the app requests no runtime permissions beyond the standard document picker. Windows: the app runs in the standard app container and reads only the files you pick. iOS / macOS: App Sandbox with user-selected file access.

App store platforms

When you install or purchase PerPlan, Apple, Google, or Microsoft process your purchase and may share aggregated, anonymous install and crash statistics with us through their developer consoles. These are governed by their own privacy policies. We do not receive your name, email address, or payment details from them.

Support messages

If you contact support by email, your email provider sends the message and whatever details you choose to include. Support correspondence is used only to answer your request and may be kept as long as needed to maintain an accurate support history. You may ask us to delete it at any time.

Children

PerPlan is a professional construction tool and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Retention and deletion

App data stays on your device until you delete a project or uninstall the app. Deleting a project removes its working PDF copy and all data attached to it. The original PDF you imported remains wherever you stored it, under your control.

Website

This website does not use advertising cookies or behavioral analytics. Its hosting provider (GitHub Pages) may process basic technical request information, such as an IP address and browser type, to deliver and protect the site.
