What PerPlan is

PerPlan is a plan-reading, navigation, and note-taking tool for construction PDFs. It helps you find sheets, notes, and references, take reference measurements, and organize questions and RFIs. It is not a design, engineering, estimating, or code-compliance tool, and it never replaces the stamped drawings, the specifications, or the professionals responsible for them.

Dimensions govern

Every number PerPlan derives — scaled measurements, stair and roof math, on-center counts, quantities — is a reference value calculated from drawing geometry or from numbers you type. Written dimensions on the plans, field conditions, and the stamped drawings always govern. Verify before cutting, ordering, or doing structural work. Snap, sheet identification, reference verification, and door/window reconciliation are aids that can be wrong on dense, skewed, scanned, or unusual drawings; a "no result" never proves something is absent.
