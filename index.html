How do I add a plan set?

From Projects, tap the plus button, name the project, and choose a PDF from Files. PerPlan creates a local project around that plan set.

Why does search skip some pages?

Search uses text embedded in the PDF. Scanned image-only pages may not contain searchable text and are identified honestly as unavailable for text search.

How do I replace a PDF?

Open the project and choose Replace PDF. Select the updated plan set from Files, then verify the page count and drawings.

Does PerPlan verify construction details?

No. PerPlan is a plan-reference and navigation tool. Always verify dimensions, specifications, and construction details against the stamped plan set and applicable requirements.

Privacy

Private by default.

Effective July 24, 2026

The short version

PerPlan does not require an account. The app does not include advertising, tracking, cloud sync, or third-party analytics. Project documents and search processing remain on your device.

Information the app processes

PerPlan processes the PDF files you choose, project names, page information, and local search indexes to provide its features. This information is stored locally on your device and is not sent to PerPlan servers.

Collection and sharing

PerPlan does not collect or sell personal information through the app. The app does not share project documents or usage data with advertising networks, analytics providers, or data brokers.

Support messages

If you contact support, your email provider sends the message and any details you choose to include. Support correspondence is used only to answer your request and may be retained as needed to maintain an accurate support history.

Website information

This website does not use advertising cookies or behavioral analytics. Its hosting provider may process basic technical request information, such as an IP address and browser type, to deliver and protect the site.

Retention and deletion

App data remains on your device until you delete a project or the app. A source PDF stored in Files remains under your control. You may request deletion of support correspondence by contacting us.

Changes and contact

If PerPlan’s data practices change, this policy will be updated before those changes take effect. For privacy questions or deletion requests, email clydesroll@gmail.com.
